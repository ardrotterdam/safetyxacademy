What Is the NEBOSH IGC? Cost, Duration and Career Value (2026)

20 January 2025 · updated 5 August 2026 · SafetyXAcademy Team

If you're looking to build a career in health and safety — or take your HSE career international — one qualification comes up again and again: the NEBOSH IGC. But what exactly is it, what does it cost, and is it worth the investment? Here's the complete picture.

NEBOSH IGC in a nutshell

NEBOSH stands for the National Examination Board in Occupational Safety and Health, a UK-based examination board that sets the global standard for health and safety qualifications. The International General Certificate (IGC) is its flagship credential: an internationally recognised foundation qualification in occupational health and safety.

The IGC is recognised in more than 130 countries and is a hard requirement in HSE job postings across international sectors — oil & gas, offshore, construction, logistics and maritime. Where national qualifications are mainly recognised at home, the NEBOSH IGC opens doors across borders. For a deeper dive into cost, exam format and study time, see our NEBOSH IGC complete guide 2026.

Who is the NEBOSH IGC for?

The IGC is an entry-level qualification at a serious professional standard. Typical candidates include:

  • Professionals moving into an HSE role (safety officer, HSE advisor, QHSE coordinator)
  • Managers and supervisors with health and safety responsibilities
  • Technicians and operators in industry, offshore or construction who want a stronger international CV
  • Career changers entering the safety profession

There are no formal entry requirements. Exams are taken in English, so a reasonable command of professional English is needed.

What you'll learn: the two units

The NEBOSH IGC consists of two units:

IG1 — Management of Health and Safety

The theoretical foundation: safety management systems, international standards (based on ILO frameworks), risk assessment methodology, safety culture, and measuring and improving performance. IG1 concludes with an open-book examination completed remotely within 24 hours. Note that the exam format was revised in 2026 — read more about the new NEBOSH IGC exam 2026.

IG2 — Risk Assessment

The practical component: you carry out a full risk assessment of a real workplace. You identify hazards — from machinery safety and hazardous substances to ergonomics and fire — evaluate the risks and propose control measures. IG2 is a practical assignment, not an exam.

How long does it take?

The official study load is roughly 110 to 130 hours. How you spread those hours depends on the study format:

  • Classroom: typically an intensive programme of 10 to 12 training days, plus self-study
  • Online / self-paced: entirely at your own pace; most learners complete the course in 3 to 6 months alongside a full-time job

What does the NEBOSH IGC cost?

Costs vary widely by format and provider. Classroom programmes in Western Europe typically run €2,000 to €3,500 including exams. Many employers reimburse the course — a certified safety professional adds direct value on site and counts in tenders and audits.

What is the qualification worth on the job market?

What the NEBOSH IGC delivers:

  • International recognition — the key difference compared to purely national qualifications; see also NEBOSH vs MVK if you are comparing with the Dutch MVK route
  • Access to HSE roles in sectors where the certificate is a job requirement
  • A clear progression path — the IGC is the stepping stone to the NEBOSH International Diploma and professional memberships such as IOSH (TechIOSH)
  • Salary impact — HSE roles requiring NEBOSH typically pay noticeably better than comparable roles without; explore our HSE salary guide for the Netherlands 2026

Frequently asked questions

Is the NEBOSH IGC difficult?

The level is comparable to a solid vocational/early-degree standard. With proper preparation, pass rates are high; the IG1 open-book exam tests understanding and application, not memorisation.

Is the exam available in other languages?

The exams are in English. Good guidance in your own language during preparation makes a real difference.

How long is the certificate valid?

The NEBOSH IGC does not expire. Employers do, however, expect you to keep your knowledge current.

Can I complete the NEBOSH IGC fully online?

Yes. The study programme, the IG1 open-book exam (remote) and the IG2 practical assignment can all be completed online.
