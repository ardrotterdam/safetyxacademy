Context

Why VCA in English?

Many industrial employers in Rotterdam and Rijnmond work with multilingual teams. Where English is the shared working language, English VCA preparation helps people follow safety terminology, risks and responsibilities clearly — without everyone needing Dutch first.

Relevant in terminals, logistics, maintenance, technical contracting, construction, petrochemical services, offshore-related contractors and industrial projects around the port. Check where needed whether your worksite or client accepts English: that can differ by site or client.

Audience

Who is an English VCA course for?

For people and organisations that need safety certificates, and where English is the practical training or team language.

  • International operational employees
  • Foreign contractors and technical contractors
  • English-speaking technicians and maintenance staff
  • Supervisors and team leaders
  • Expats in industrial roles
  • Temporary workers and project staff
  • Multinational project teams
  • Employers and staffing agencies with multilingual teams

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about VCA in English

Can I take VCA training in English?

SafetyXAcademy is preparing VCA training in English in Rotterdam and Rijnmond for international employees, supervisors and contractors. Confirmed public course dates are not yet available. Register your interest with no obligation.

Is B-VCA available in English?

B-VCA in English is in preparation. It is intended for international operational employees who need fundamental VCA safety knowledge. Share your preference when you register your interest.

Is VOL-VCA available in English?

VOL-VCA in English is also in preparation, aimed at English-speaking operational supervisors, team leaders and supervisors. The right certificate depends on your role and the requirements of your client or worksite.

Who is an English VCA course for?

For international operational employees, foreign contractors, English-speaking technicians, supervisors, expats in industrial roles, temporary workers, multinational project teams and employers with multilingual teams in Rotterdam and Rijnmond.

Can my company organise VCA in-company training in English?

Yes, that is a key focus. English VCA in-company training for international teams is in preparation: one team, one location, with B-VCA and VOL-VCA needs in a single conversation. Exam logistics will be coordinated later through the recognised exam route. View VCA in-company.

Can the official VCA exam be taken in English?

Training and exam are separate. Whether the official exam can be taken in English depends on the recognised VCA exam route and available exam options. SafetyXAcademy is preparing the English training route and plans to align official exam coordination later via the recognised exam system. English exam availability through SafetyXAcademy is not yet confirmed. Also check the requirements of your worksite or client where relevant. Read more about the VCA exam in Rotterdam.

Is an English VCA certificate the same certificate?

Yes. After a successful recognised exam you receive the official B-VCA or VOL-VCA certificate via the recognised exam route. The language of preparation does not change the certificate type — it remains the same personal VCA diploma.

How long is a VCA certificate valid?

After a successful recognised exam, a B-VCA or VOL-VCA certificate is valid for ten years.

When do the first English VCA courses start in Rotterdam?

English VCA training is in preparation. Final public start dates and prices are not yet available. You can register your interest with no obligation — free, with no payment and no final enrolment.

Does SafetyXAcademy issue the official certificate itself?

No. The official VCA exam and certificate run through a recognised exam body. SafetyXAcademy prepares the training and delivers it with qualified trainers.

Which VCA do I need: B-VCA or VOL-VCA?

B-VCA is for operational employees. VOL-VCA is for operational supervisors who are also responsible for the people they lead. The right certificate depends on your role and the requirements of your client or worksite.
